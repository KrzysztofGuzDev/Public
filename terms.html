The service

TAKE coaches you while you take photographs: one instruction at a time, a score on the result, optional on-device finishing, and optional voice planning. It does not replace a professional photographer and it does not promise a particular look, score, or outcome for every scene.

Photographs you capture belong to you. TAKE does not claim copyright in your pictures.

Free use and Take PRO

New and returning users receive five completed photo sessions each calendar month at no charge. A session is spent only when TAKE actually delivers a photograph — not when you open the camera, and not when you retry the same shot.

Take PRO is an auto-renewable Apple subscription that unlocks unlimited sessions, every scene and style, voice planning, scoring, and finishing.

  • Monthly: USD 4.99 per month (or the equivalent Apple shows in your storefront).
  • Yearly: USD 29.99 per year (or the equivalent Apple shows in your storefront).

Auto-renewal

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. The subscription renews automatically unless you turn it off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the period. Manage or cancel any time in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
